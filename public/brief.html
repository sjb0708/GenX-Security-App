Meet & Greet / Gift Policy

Day-of Coordination
Meet & greets occur after the show, so the time cannot be pre-scheduled. The tour manager determines the meet & greet location on the day of show, and the venue determines the post-show timing. Once the location is set, Lead Security is responsible for arranging a walkthrough with venue security to review on-site security and M&G protocols.
Protocol is determined day-of by the tour manager and Lead Security during the on-site security walkthrough.
Gift policy is finalized by the tour manager and Lead Security during the on-site security walkthrough.
